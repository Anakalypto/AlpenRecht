## Worst answers

- **T084 (1, unchanged):** It still tells the user that the complaint deadline is the art. 31 CP "prescription" of about ten years, when it is 3 months. A victim who followed this would lose the right to complain. It also wrongly ties the 5-year penalty to public gatherings. The cantonal half (expropriation, river damage) is irrelevant.
- **T090 (1):** It still answers as if the user were a public authority. A private person filming the street is governed by the DSG (EDÖB: public space must essentially not be filmed), ZGB 28 and 179quater. The answer tells them to get a communal regulation homologated instead.
- **T093 (1):** It uses the lump-sum taxation regime and joint spousal liability as the "federal principles" of wealth tax, and never says that there is no federal wealth tax.
- **T091 (1):** The deduction list is now broad, but it states that Switzerland has no communal taxes, which is false.
- **T092 (1):** Truncated, has no TI cantonal figures, and lists the imputed rental value as a deduction.
