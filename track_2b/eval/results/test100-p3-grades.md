## Worst answers

- **T044 (0) — Grenzgänger KV.** The yes/no answer is wrong. For the main real-world case (EU/EFTA commuters) Swiss insurance applies by default with an option to exempt; the bot inverts this by reading KVV Art. 3 (residual non-EU rule) as the general rule. A user following it could miss the 3-month Optionsrecht deadline or stay uninsured.
- **T059 (1) — Stepchild adoption.** Wrong article (264a joint adoption instead of 264c) and wrong procedure (international-adoption documents from AdoV). The 28-year age rule and "3 years household as spouses" do not apply this way. The legally key 2018 opening to cohabiting couples is missing.
- **T043 (1) — Retirement age.** Grounds the answer on the LPP invalidity-pension article and ignores the AHV 21 reform. This is exactly the 2023/2024 reform a user would need (women born 1961–63 have transitional reference ages).
- Close behind: T052 (spouse ranked after all relatives in the summary), T047 (misses the income threshold / full-allowance rule for part-timers), T049 (incomplete coverage list + wrong authority).

## Recurring failure patterns

1. **Retrieval drives the answer, not the question.** When the top hit is a neighbouring article (LPP 24 for retirement age, KVV 3 for Grenzgänger, LFA for family allowances, AdoV 7 for stepchild adoption), the model faithfully paraphrases it and gives a wrong or skewed answer. It doesn't check whether the cited article is the governing one.
2. **Paraphrase without the key figure.** Even when the right article is retrieved (AVIG 13 in T041, LAFam 13 al. 3 in T047), the decisive number is left out (12 months of contributions, income threshold). Answers list conditions but rarely give amounts, rates or deadlines a lawyer would lead with.
3. **Narrow scope on broad questions.** "What does IV / LAA pay" is answered with one benefit type (pensions, IJ) and misses most of the others.
4. **Hallucinated "next steps".** The process steps are not sourced and often wrong: Zivilstandsamt registration of an Ehevertrag, survivors applying to the caisse de compensation, an invented IV-Stelle address, the OFAS rent-maximum request presented to a citizen, a "recours administratif" in GE.
5. **Reforms not surfaced.** AHV 21 (women's reference age) and the 2023 inheritance revision are not mentioned, even where they're central (T043, T051).
6. **Retrieval noise in sources.** Off-topic acts get retrieved: CO2 law, biotope ordinance, 1875 Austro-Hungarian treaty, a sawing-operator CFC ordinance, LACI for LAA. Cantonal hits are often retrieved but then reported as "keine einschlägige Quelle". The template also leaves contradictions (T042 body vs footer).
7. **Latency.** 88 s average, 4 answers over 120 s (max 207 s), which is too slow for interactive use. Latency does not track quality: the fastest answer (T060, 25 s) scored the same as the slow ones.
