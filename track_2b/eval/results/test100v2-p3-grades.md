## Worst answers

- **T043 (0): retirement age.** The retrieval fix worked: AHVG 21 is now the cited source. But the model states "women at 64", which is outdated since AHV 21 and is exactly the reform users ask about, and it answers an Italian user in English. This is worse than before.
- **T044 (0): Grenzgänger KV.** Unchanged inversion of the rule. KVV Art. 3 (non-EU residual rule) is still presented as the general regime, and the EU/EFTA default subjection plus the Optionsrecht are absent.
- **T045 (1): GE premium reduction.** New regression. Retrieval now pulls LAMal 65a + ORPMUE (the procedure for insured persons living in the EU), and the model builds the user's steps on it ("commander la formule auprès de l'institution commune").
- **T046 (1): IV.** Still pension-only, with a wrong bottom of the scale (25 % instead of 40 %), an invented Art. 2 Abs. 2 IVG step, and 2003 BS Beihilfe figures dominating.
- **T048 (1): Sozialhilfe SG.** Lost the correct communal authority and kept the wrong "SR 831.0" federal claim.
- **T059 (1): stepchild adoption.** The right article (264c) is retrieved, but the 28-year joint-adoption rule is still applied.

## Remaining failure patterns

1. **Right article, wrong rule.** Fixes improved retrieval of the governing source (AHVG 21 in T043, ZGB 264c in T059, BVG 30c/WEFV in T050). But the model still mixes in rules from neighbouring articles (264a age rule, ORPMUE EU procedure, KVV 3 residual rule) or from outdated knowledge (women 64). Retrieval is no longer the only bottleneck. The model does not check which provision governs the user's case.
2. **Neighbouring-regime contamination.** EU-resident procedure for a GE resident (T045), asylum/refugee rules dominating Sozialhilfe (T048), cantonal-staff pension law for AHV age (T043), BS Beihilfe for IV (T046), TI inventory procedure for a spouse's share (T054), VD notary fees for writing a will (T053).
3. **Hallucinated or misdirected next steps.** These persist: Zivilstandsamt for an Ehevertrag (T060), cantonal instead of communal social services (T048), Inkassohilfe for the payer (T055), an "exception d'âge" procedure that does not exist (T058), "Art. 2 Abs. 2 IVG" (T046).
4. **Narrow scope on broad "what does X pay/cover" questions.** IV (T046) and LAA (T049) still cover only one benefit family.
5. **Missing key figures and reforms.** No Taggeld rate (T041), no LAFam income threshold (T047), no worked reserve fractions or mention of the 2023 revision (T051), no regime-liquidation step (T054), no public will (T053).
6. **Wrong-topic sources in the list.** Communal police regulations (Fribourg in T047/T051, Chur in T059) are now regularly retrieved, and in T059 they are even summarised in the answer. Off-topic cantonal procedure gets padded into the Kurzantwort.
7. **Template artefacts.** "Aucune source pertinente" next to cited federal sources (T042), broken citation ids ("[2ter]", "Art. 92146"), and a language slip (T043 in English).
8. **Latency got worse.** Avg 112.9 s vs 88.4 s, min 61 s, 8/20 over 120 s (max 210.7 s). Longer answers did not translate into higher scores.
