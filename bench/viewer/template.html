Alpenrecht Benchmark

100 everyday Swiss legal questions, each with a gold source checked on Fedlex or LexFind, answered twice by Apertus v1.5 70B: once by the baseline pipeline and once by the final one, where an Apertus agent searches LexFind and every answer is verified three times against its sources before it is shown.

MeasureBaselineFinal

Gold cited means the answer cites a retrieved source that is the gold article (federal) or an article of the gold act (cantonal). Times include live retrieval from Fedlex, LexFind and swisstopo on the Hack Apertus CSCS endpoint, measured with five questions running in parallel. Legal information, not legal advice.
